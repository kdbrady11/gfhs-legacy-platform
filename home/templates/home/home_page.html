Explore the Platform

The GFHS Legacy Alumni Platform brings alumni recognition, school history, historical archives, and public display content together in one shared system.

A shared home for GFHS legacy, history, and preservation.

Public visitors can explore alumni profiles, school history, archive materials, and contribution opportunities while admins manage everything from one content system.

Legacy Alumni

Search and explore alumni profiles by name, category, graduation year, achievements, and legacy.

School History

Browse historical events, traditions, milestones, photos, and stories connected to Great Falls High School.

Historical Archives

Explore yearbooks, newspapers, photos, fine arts materials, athletic records, programs, and documents.

Preservation Tools Now in Place

The platform has moved beyond a basic prototype and now includes core tools for managing historical content, uploaded media, archive records, and public discovery.

Search and Filtering

Visitors can search and filter Legacy Alumni, School History, and Historical Archives by relevant details such as names, years, categories, and document types.

Archive Management

Admins can create archive items, upload images and files, add descriptions, track source and credit information, and manage review details.

Contribution Inquiries

Community members can submit information about yearbooks, photos, newspapers, programs, and records they may want reviewed for preservation.

Submit an Inquiry

Built for the Public Website and the Kiosk

This system is designed so association admins can update content once and use it across the public website, historical archive, and touchscreen kiosk experience.

View Alumni View History View Archives

Production Foundation

The current version includes the foundation needed for a more durable, production-ready preservation platform.

Persistent Content

Site content is backed by PostgreSQL, helping preserve pages, alumni profiles, history events, archive records, and inquiry submissions.

Persistent Media

Uploaded images and files are stored through Cloudflare R2 so media can survive application redeploys and support future archive growth.

Governed Admin Workflow

Admin groups, permissions planning, archive governance fields, and review-oriented documentation support safer long-term content management.

Next Build Priorities

Future work should focus on refinement, donation integration, richer archive presentation, and stronger administrative workflows.

Donation Integration

Add a secure Stripe Checkout donation flow for the public website while keeping kiosk donations routed to a visitor’s personal device.

Richer Exhibits

Add alumni timelines, image galleries, archive collections, QR codes, captions, source notes, and curated kiosk exhibit views.

Review Workflow

Expand admin review states, content approval steps, contributor follow-up, and reporting tools for association-managed preservation work.
