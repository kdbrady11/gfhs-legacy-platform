A Living Historical Archive

This platform is being built to preserve the stories, records, photographs, documents, yearbooks, newspapers, and accomplishments connected to Great Falls High School.

Legacy Alumni

Honor graduates whose lives and achievements reflect the school’s lasting impact.

School History

Preserve the moments, traditions, and milestones that define Great Falls High School.

Historical Archives

Make yearbooks, newspapers, photos, records, and documents easier to find and explore.

Built for the Public Website and the Kiosk

This system is designed so association admins can update content once and use it across the public website, historical archive, and touchscreen kiosk experience.

Prototype Roadmap

This prototype establishes the foundation for a shared historical preservation system. Future phases can expand the platform with richer archives, deeper search, media galleries, and a fully enhanced kiosk experience.

Current Prototype

Public website, admin-managed pages, alumni profiles, school history, archive foundation, donation page, and kiosk mode.

Next Build Phase

Add photos, alumni timelines, document uploads, archive search, QR codes, and improved homepage feature controls.

Long-Term Vision

A permanent digital monument for Great Falls High School history, legacy alumni, records, yearbooks, newspapers, and public displays.
